# Public copy rules — weai.solutions

Status: Active · Owner rulings of 2026-11-07 / 2026-11-08 · Supersedes `docs/guardrails/landing-claims-policy.md` (retired).

This repo is the public facade. It exists so investors and people looking for a solution can see what WEAI is building, how clearly it thinks, and how far each product has got. Keep copy simple and honest. Six rules.

1. **Pain points, not mechanisms, not prices.** Each product says what problem the visitor has and the direction WEAI is taking. It does not explain internals and shows no pricing.
2. **Progress is stated by public version only.** `v0.x` = development-stage at its confirmed version; `v1.0` = formally launched into operation. A version appears only when the Owner has confirmed it in `src/content/products.ts`. Unknown facts are omitted — never `TODO`, never a dash, never an invented date.
3. **Nothing is claimed as running unless it is.** "Works now" lists only confirmed items. Planned connections and capabilities are labelled `planned`. Design intent is written as intent ("so that…", "is designed to…"), not as live behaviour.
4. **Names are public; codenames are not.** The six lines (Foundation · Ops · Workforce · Payments · Platforms · Contact) and the product names (CoStudio, DomainOps, OpsBoard, MailOps, HR-AR OS, the platforms) may be named and their purpose described. Foundation may name Governance, Harness, Runtime, Gateway, Security Shield and refer to the kernel once. Internal component codenames, repository internals and private mechanics stay out.
5. **No real-business attribution without Owner ruling.** Platforms are described as WEAI-built only where the Owner has said so; otherwise the record stays neutral.
6. **No commercial surfaces, no AI chat, no analytics** on this site. Contact is a form that shows success only on confirmed delivery.

Homepage body is frozen until the section pages are complete; its tonality (warm paper, ink, one terracotta accent) is the standard for every page.
